# NestedApps | A Wordpress Plugin

I was learning Lua/Love2D and this was my first intro: [DONDEV PONG](https://donalcasey.io/2026/10/22/love-pong/ )
More exciting; I have been making a lot of games in react and Love2d to teach me spanish; I created a WP plugin where I can upload zips of the react apps and love apps so that WP could unpack, envoke the right libraries and let me shortcode embed these apps on any page- turning WP into a cpanel for nested apps. With this, I hope to push up way more sandbox items in a few different languages deployed to my own DONDEV site. Some of the cool to me items include:

- A daily journal that includes a tarot reading and log. 
- Time tracking, pomodoro timer, and a daily journal.
- Budgeting and expense tracking with a daily journal.
- More Spanish learning games and flashcards.
- BuenasNora! A pollypocket-esque game where I have recreated my daughter's room complete with interactive objects from real life. Program her sound machine, play and compose with the xylophone, read books, and get ready for school!

## ES WordMunchers

You might remember an old computer game for learning Math and English called "WordMunchers" or "MathMunchers"; you were an alien crossing a checkerboard, evading monsters, and "munching" on the correct answers to victory! I have recreated a simplistic version of this childhood joy but I wired it up to my SpanishDictionary.sh - This is a whole other topic. I made a CLI interface for translating ES -> EN and vice versa. If I look something up, it saves the words and phrases as individual flashcards. If a card exists, I denote in analytics that I need to work harder on that card. So now, I have an evolving journal that reports progress and is catered to the topics of conversation I run into day to day. Anyway, here is one of the games embedded through my "NestedApps" plugin for Wordpress! [Play ES WordMunchers](https://donalcasey.io/2026/11/10/es-word-munchers/)
